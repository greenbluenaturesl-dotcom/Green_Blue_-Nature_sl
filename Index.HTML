About Our Company

Nature Inspired. Quality Driven.

Green Blue Nature is a Sri Lankan herbal cosmetics manufacturing company established in 2019. Our vision is to bring the natural benefits of local herbs and botanical ingredients into everyday beauty care.

We focus on developing herbal personal care products inspired by traditional knowledge and modern manufacturing practices.
